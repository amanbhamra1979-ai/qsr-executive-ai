QSR Executive AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

1. Overview

QSR Executive AI is an application designed to assist restaurant and QSR professionals with generating, reviewing, managing, and publishing professional business content.

2. LinkedIn Integration

QSR Executive AI may use LinkedIn's APIs and OAuth authentication to connect a user's LinkedIn account to the application.

The LinkedIn connection is initiated only after the user explicitly authorizes the application through LinkedIn's authorization process.

3. Information We Access

When a user connects LinkedIn, QSR Executive AI may receive information necessary to authenticate the user and perform authorized actions, including the user's LinkedIn member identifier and authorization credentials provided through LinkedIn OAuth.

The application uses only the permissions granted by the user and the LinkedIn APIs.

4. How We Use Information

LinkedIn information is used only to provide the functionality authorized by the user, including connecting the user's LinkedIn account and publishing approved content on the user's behalf.

QSR Executive AI does not sell LinkedIn user information or use it for unrelated advertising purposes.

5. Content Publishing

QSR Executive AI may publish content to the authenticated user's LinkedIn profile when the user has authorized the LinkedIn connection and the content has been approved through the application's workflow.

The application does not publish content to a user's LinkedIn profile without the required authorization.

6. Storage and Security

Authentication information required to maintain the LinkedIn connection may be stored securely by the application. Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect stored information against unauthorized access.

7. Data Retention

LinkedIn connection information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the authorized integration or until the user disconnects the LinkedIn account, subject to applicable legal or operational requirements.

8. Disconnecting LinkedIn

Users may disconnect their LinkedIn account from QSR Executive AI. Upon disconnection, the application will no longer use the stored LinkedIn authorization to perform actions on the user's behalf.

9. Third-Party Services

QSR Executive AI may interact with third-party services, including LinkedIn, to provide functionality requested by the user. Use of those services is also subject to their respective terms and privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to QSR Executive AI, its integrations, or applicable requirements. Updates will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy or QSR Executive AI, please contact the application owner through the contact information associated with the application.
